## What Is This?

AI4SWE is an end-to-end AI engineering guide built specifically for software engineers who already know how to code — and want to understand AI systems the right way.

Not a surface-level overview. Not a marketing story. A technically honest, top-down walkthrough of modern AI from first principles to production systems.

The goal is to produce engineers who can reason clearly about the full stack — from a chat interface all the way down to the model, the inference runtime, and the hardware beneath it.

---

## Who Is This For?

Software engineers who:

- Are familiar with CS fundamentals and can write code
- Keep hearing terms like LLM, RAG, agents, fine-tuning — and want to understand what they actually mean, at the right technical depth
- Are being asked to "add AI" to systems and want to make informed architectural decisions
- Want a structured path from zero to being able to build and reason about real AI-powered systems

This is **not** for beginners to programming. The material assumes engineering maturity and moves accordingly.

---

## The Approach

**Top-down.** Start with the full map — the terminology, the history, the stack — before going deep on any single topic. Every lecture gives you the "why this exists" before the "how it works."

**Technically rich.** Analogies are grounded in CS concepts you already know. The depth is calibrated for engineers, not product managers.

**Layered.** Modern AI is a stack. Knowing which layer you're operating at — product, API, model, systems, hardware — determines what tools you use, what decisions you make, and what constraints you're working within. Every lecture is explicit about where it sits in the stack.

**Build-oriented.** The series is designed so that by the end, you're not just familiar with AI concepts — you're equipped to build, evaluate, and reason about AI systems end-to-end.

---

## Topics Covered

The series covers the full arc of modern AI engineering, roughly in this order:

**Foundations**
- The AI landscape and correct terminology — from classical AI to modern LLMs
- Historical context: symbolic AI, ML, deep learning, and the transformer breakthrough
- The full LLM stack: product layer down to hardware

**Working with LLMs**
- How to interact with models at each layer of abstraction
- Prompt engineering — techniques, patterns, and applied problem solving
- How LLMs actually work — tokens, inference, context, decoding

**Building on LLMs**
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Vector databases — the storage layer behind semantic search
- Fine-tuning and when to use it vs. RAG vs. prompting

**AI Systems**
- Agentic systems — LLMs that use tools, memory, and decision loops
- Orchestration patterns and frameworks
- Evaluation — how to know if your system actually works

**Going Deeper** *(planned)*
- Training dynamics and scaling
- Inference optimization
- Production AI engineering considerations
